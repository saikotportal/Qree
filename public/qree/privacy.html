Privacy, in plain language.

QREE is designed so QR content can be processed directly in your browser. This page explains what that means without making claims beyond what the product can support.

Browser processing

When you create a QR code, the content you enter is processed by the QREE interface and its code-generation libraries in your browser. We do not need that content to be uploaded to a QREE server for the generator to work.

ImportantBrowser-based processing describes how the generator is designed to work. Your browser still makes network requests for the page and any resources it loads.

What may be stored on your device

QREE may use browser storage for interface preferences such as your light or dark theme. If you choose to save a style, those style settings can also be stored locally. These settings do not represent a server account.

You can remove browser-stored settings by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Third-party resources

The page may load fonts, scripts, or other resources from third-party providers. Those providers can receive normal technical request information, such as your IP address and browser details, when your browser requests a resource. Their own policies apply. QREE does not control those providers.

Links and QR scans

A QR code contains the content you choose to encode. If it contains a link, scanning software may open that link. QREE does not operate a redirect or scan analytics service for static codes.

Changes and questions

This policy may change as QREE changes. The date above will be updated when the wording is revised.

Questions about this page can be sent through the contact page.
